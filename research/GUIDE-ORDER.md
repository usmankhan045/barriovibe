## What this changed

The first draft led with the cross-border flagship on the grounds that it is
the most defensible piece. The data disagrees: SECP company registration has
four times the query mass and a weaker page one, and it is the entry point to
the whole compliance funnel. A reader who registers a company then needs an
NTN, then a sales tax number, then annual filings, and every one of those is a
guide in this set.

PSEB ranks seventh despite 150 queries, which is the useful surprise. Its page
one is the most contested in the set (weakness 0.8): established players hold
it, so it needs the surrounding cluster to link into it rather than to carry
the cluster alone.

## Deadline context

Written 10 September 2026, twenty days before the 30 September filing deadline.
Head terms will not rank in twenty days. What is realistic: indexing starts,
long-tail queries land, and the October extension tail is substantial. FBR
extended twice in TY2025, to 15 then 31 October, and its 5.9 million figure was
measured on 31 October.

That argues for publishing the filer and NTN guides inside the window even
though SECP scores higher, because those two are the ones with a season. SECP,
trademark and property are flat year-round and lose nothing by shipping in
October.
